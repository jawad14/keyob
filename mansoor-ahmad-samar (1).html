A career shipped in versions.

Engineering philosophy

Four rules the code answers to.

01

Boring is a feature

The most valuable systems are the ones nobody talks about, because they simply work. Novelty belongs in the product, not in the plumbing. Proven tools, ruthlessly applied.

02

Own the whole lifecycle

Code that can't be maintained wasn't finished — it was abandoned early. Every system KEYOB ships is designed to be operated, extended and understood long after launch day.

03

AI earns its place

Automation and AI go where they demonstrably remove work or risk — not where they demo well. If it can't be measured in the client's operation, it doesn't ship.

04

The team is the architecture

System quality is a lagging indicator of team quality. Reviews, standards and honest technical debate aren't process overhead — they're how reliability is actually manufactured.
