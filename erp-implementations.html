02 & 03 · The platforms

Two paths. The right one depends on your business.

Both are capable, proven ERP platforms with very different characters. Part of our job is telling you plainly which one fits — and occasionally, that neither does.

Platform 02

SAP Business One

SAP's ERP built for small and mid-sized businesses — a tightly integrated, finance-first core backed by SAP's global ecosystem.

Typically the better fit when
  • →Finance and compliance rigour lead the requirement
  • →You want a single vendor-backed suite with a mature partner network
  • →Processes suit a proven standard more than heavy customisation
  • →You may grow into the wider SAP ecosystem over time
Explore SAP Business One → Implementation · migration · support
Platform 03

Odoo

A modular open-source business suite — a broad range of apps and an architecture built to be extended around how you actually work.

Typically the better fit when
  • →Your processes are distinctive and need real flexibility
  • →You want to start with a few apps and expand module by module
  • →Open-source extensibility and lower licence entry matter
  • →Tight custom integration with existing tools is central

How we deliver

The same discipline, whichever platform wins.

Platforms differ. The way a serious implementation is run does not.

Stage 01

Discover

Understand the business, the current landscape and the value at stake — then recommend a platform, including the honest answer when neither is right.

Stage 02

Design

Fit-to-standard workshops against your real processes. Gaps identified honestly, integration architecture and data strategy set early.

Stage 03

Build

Iterative configuration, extensions and integrations — validated with real users in short cycles, never one distant demo at the end.

Stage 04

Migrate

Data cleansing, mapping and rehearsed migration runs. Data quality is treated as its own workstream, not a task someone squeezes in.

Stage 05

Go live

Cutover, readiness checks, training and launch support — the unglamorous discipline that decides how go-live week actually feels.

Stage 06

Run & improve

Hypercare into steady-state support, then continuous improvement — the stage where the investment finally compounds.
