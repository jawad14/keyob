The short answer

What is demand generation?

Definition

Demand generation is the work of creating awareness and trust among people who are not ready to buy yet, so that when they are ready, you are the business they already have a view about. It covers content, proof, distribution and the capture path that lets interest become an enquiry.

It is different from lead generation, which collects contact details from people already looking. Demand generation builds the pool that lead generation draws from. Businesses that only run lead generation find the pool keeps getting more expensive.

Why it matters

Consistency without a point is just noise.

Three reasons most B2B social activity produces attention but not enquiries.

01

The content has no position

Generic industry posts could come from any competitor. Content only compounds when it is recognisably yours and says something specific.

02

There is no proof layer

People buy from businesses they believe can do the work. Case studies, client stories and real results do more than volume of posts ever will.

03

There is nowhere to land

Interest arrives and finds no obvious next step. A profile that leads nowhere converts attention into nothing.

How we work

Build the engine, then feed it.

Content is the visible part. Most of the value comes from the decisions made before anything is published.

Stage 01

Define the audience and the point of view

Who you are talking to and what you believe that is worth hearing. Content without a point of view is content nobody remembers.

Stage 02

Build the proof library

Client work, results and stories captured properly. This becomes the backbone of credible content for the next year.

Stage 03

Plan the content system

Themes, formats, channels and a realistic cadence. Realistic matters: a plan nobody can sustain is worse than a smaller one that runs.

Stage 04

Build the capture path

Landing pages, forms and CRM connection, so an interested reader has a clear, tracked next step.

Stage 05

Publish and distribute

Consistent output across the channels where your buyers actually are, rather than all of them at once.

Stage 06

Measure what converts

Which themes and formats produce enquiries, not just impressions. The plan follows the evidence.
